CloudForArt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy explains how CloudForArt Pin Planner handles data. CloudForArt Pin Planner is a private tool used only by the owner of the CloudForArt Etsy shop (cloudforart.etsy.com) and the Pinterest business account Cloud-B4Art. It is not offered to the public or to other Pinterest users.

What data we access

With the owner's permission through Pinterest's official OAuth login, the tool accesses only the owner's own Pinterest account information, boards, Pins and basic Pin analytics. It does not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest user.

How we use it

The data is used only to create and schedule Pins for the owner's own Etsy listings and to show the owner how those Pins perform.

Storage and security

We never ask for or store Pinterest passwords or session cookies. The OAuth access token is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings (Security > Apps), and all locally stored data is deleted on request.

Contact

For any privacy question, email cloud4art06@gmail.com.
